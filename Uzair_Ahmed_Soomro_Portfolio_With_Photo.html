About Me

A developer who loves to build

Get to know the person behind the code.

Hi, I'm Uzair Ahmed Soomro, a Full Stack Developer based in Pakistan. My journey into programming began with a simple curiosity: how do websites actually work? That curiosity quickly turned into a passion for building things for the web.

I enjoy working across the entire stack — from crafting responsive, accessible interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, to building robust backends with Node.js, Express and databases like MongoDB and MySQL. I'm also fascinated by low-level programming and have built academic projects in 8086 Assembly Language.

My approach to problem-solving is simple: understand the problem deeply, break it into small pieces, and build clean, maintainable solutions. I believe great software is a balance of performance, usability and thoughtful design.

Right now, I'm focused on sharpening my full-stack skills, contributing to open-source, and building projects that make a real difference. I'm always learning, always shipping, and always looking for the next challenge.
